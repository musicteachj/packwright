PACKWRIGHT MARK · ROUND 2 · REFINING 1c

Tick height

Three tick heights on the 32 px master; ring, arms and stroke unchanged. Tick heights are whole units so the ends stay sharp: 6, 8 and 10 of 32. At 16 px a tick has to be an odd number of pixels to center on the 1 px line, so 19% and 25% both come out at 3 px and 31% at 5 px. Ink stays chrome-100: 15.17:1 on chrome-950, 14.10:1 on chrome-900.

Recommendation · 2b, 25%

At 8 of 32 units the ticks are under half the ring's 18-unit diameter, so the ring stays the subject and the ticks read as the ends of a line, as on the canvas callouts. 2c keeps a 5 px tick at 16 px, which is still more than half the ring there, and the wing shape returns. 2a is identical to 2b at 16 px and only a 6 px tick at 32 px, close enough to the 2 px line that it begins to read as a plain cross.

2b · 16 px · 12×

Redrawn for 16 px. The ring is a single-pixel 9 px circle with no doubled steps on the diagonals. Arms and line are 1 px on column and row 7. Ticks are 3 px, centered on the line, with two pixels of line between each tick and the ring.

The whole mark turns as one piece, clockwise, at a constant 2.4 s per revolution (150°/s), linear, with no easing. The ring shows no motion; the arms and ticks carry it. The mark repeats every 180°, so the visible cycle is 1.2 s. Spin the 32 px master at every size, including 16 px: the pixel drawing only holds at 0° and 90°. Under prefers-reduced-motion the mark stays still and the "Loading" label carries the state.

PACKWRIGHT MARK · ROUND 1

Four directions

Every mark is one color, chrome-100 #ebe7e0: 15.17:1 on chrome-950 and 14.10:1 on chrome-900, against the 3:1 non-text minimum. Notice blue is held back this round. The 16 px drawings are separate pixel drawings on a 15-pixel grid, so 1 px strokes have a true center pixel; the last row and column are empty. 32 and 64 px use the 32 px master.

Lockups are IBM Plex Sans 600 at 14 px beside the 16 px drawing, 8 px apart, then at 2×. Recommendation: Packwright, capitalized. It matches how the name appears in running text, and the cap P gives the mark a straight vertical to sit against.

1a
Hairline target
lead concept

The registration mark as printers draw it: one circle, with a crosshair running past it on all four sides. At 16 px the circle is plotted pixel by pixel at radius 5 so it stays round without blur. It repeats every 90°, so as a loader the turn shows only between quarter positions.
